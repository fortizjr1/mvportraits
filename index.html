05 Good to know

Questions,
answered.

How soon will we get the photos?+

I can usually have a first set ready in a few days, with the full gallery delivered within a few weeks depending on the project.

We don’t know how to pose. Can you help?+

Absolutely. I’ll guide you throughout the session so the process feels relaxed, natural, and fun.

Do you travel for shoots?+

Yes. I’m based in the San Francisco Bay Area and available throughout the USA and worldwide. Travel fees may apply depending on the location.

What does booking look like?+

We’ll start with a free consultation. Once we agree on the date and scope, a signed contract and prepayment secure your booking.
